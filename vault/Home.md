## Areas
Each link is a hub. Replace these with your own life areas. `/session-log` creates the hub the first time it logs a session for that area, and every later session links back to it. That is how the graph grows clusters.

## Rhythm
- End of a meaningful session: `/session-log`
- Every week: `/weekly`, which writes to `90 Agent Output/Weekly/`
- Every month: `/lint`

## Zones
- `10 Sources/`: raw material (read-only to the agent)
- `20 Notes/`: **your own writing**, the part that matters most
- `90 Agent Output/`: everything AI-written
